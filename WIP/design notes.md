# Design Notes

## Direction

The revised website uses a cleaner blue and white business services aesthetic with Plus Jakarta Sans headings and DM Sans body text. The header uses the supplied transparent Simtrak logo directly.

## Hero

The headline now introduces Simtrak as an online business consultancy and uses a shorter two part statement so the first screen feels stronger and easier to scan.

The gap below the navigation is reduced. The hero visual keeps the floating service idea but removes the earlier explanatory labels from the center. The official logo is now the only center element.

## About and motion

About Simtrak is the first content section after the hero. It combines established company information with the supplied founder photo and a four number KPI row.

Abstract moving vector paths are used through the About, services, testimonials and footer sections. The colourful moving hero widgets remain intact.

## Service cards

Four large cards communicate the core services. Six compact colourful controls present the add on services. Every control sends the visitor to the enquiry form with the selected service checkbox already chosen.

The core cards contain practical deliverables while the add on cards stay compact and animated.

## Founder

The founder area now uses the original supplied photo of Simran Sharma with verified founder details and a direct LinkedIn link.

## Client trust

The client logo section uses two animated rows on a light background and appears at the end of the main page. The testimonial section uses the supplied 5.0 Google rating and five reviewer names alongside the published SAWEC client experience.

## Footer

The footer uses a light blue layered layout with a prominent conversation panel, animated vector lines, the transparent logo, branded social buttons and an expandable services menu.

## Responsive behavior

The website has dedicated desktop, tablet and mobile layouts for the hero graphic, service cards, founder content, blogs, enquiry form and footer.
