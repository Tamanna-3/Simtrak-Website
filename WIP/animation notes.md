# Animation Notes

## Motion used

The hero uses slow grid movement, soft background glow, orbit movement and floating service labels.

Pointer parallax moves hero elements only on devices with a precise pointer.

Buttons respond with subtle magnetic movement.

Headings reveal word by word and content rises gently into view.

The client logo row moves continuously and pauses on hover.

Service cards lift slightly on hover and their action arrows move forward.

## Accessibility

All continuous and interactive animation is reduced when the visitor has enabled reduced motion.

Pointer based effects do not run on touch devices.

Motion does not block access to content or controls.
