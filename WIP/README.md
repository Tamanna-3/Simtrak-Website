# Simtrak Solutions Website WIP Notes

## What was completed

The website now uses a permanent white navigation bar with only Home, About, Our Services, Blogs, Contact Us and Get A Quote.

The hero has shorter spacing, search friendly business service content and the headline Smart Business Services For Stronger Growth. The center graphic now uses only the official Simtrak logo.

The discontinued service remains removed from the full website. The service structure now follows the approved core and add on service list.

About Simtrak is the first content section after the hero and states that the company was established in 2020. It now uses the supplied photo of founder Simran Sharma and includes the requested business numbers for team members, excellence, services and clients.

The services follow About Simtrak and are separated into four detailed core services and six focused add on services. Every service control selects the matching checkbox in the enquiry form.

The testimonials section now includes the published SAWEC testimonial, a 5.0 rating summary and all five supplied Google reviewers. The broken first blog link was removed.

The client logo section now appears at the end of the main page. The footer uses a more expressive light blue layout with a strong conversation panel, the transparent logo, office address and branded Instagram, LinkedIn and WhatsApp icons.

The enquiry form allows visitors to choose multiple services, validates all fields and opens WhatsApp with a prepared message using the verified Simtrak number.

## How to run the website

Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

For the best local experience, open a terminal in the project folder and run:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8080`.
